## ✨ Why Combo Bot?

Ever wanted to vent, confess to your crush, or just chat with someone new—without the hassle of setting up burner accounts everywhere?

**Combo Bot brings together two essential features that usually require separate bots, all into one seamless experience:**

| | |
|---|---|
| 💌 | Send secret messages to channels completely anonymously |
| 🔍 | Chat 1-on-1 with random strangers with total privacy |

No need to install multiple bots or sign up twice. Everything works directly from a single chat window.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 💌 Menfess — Spill the Tea, Stay Incognito
Type `mfs!` + your message, and boom—your post goes live on the channel with no name, no username, and no paper trail.

- 📝 Supports text, 🖼️ photos, 🎥 videos, and 🎙️ voice notes.
- 👀 Preview your post before sending to catch any typos.
- ⏱️ Set an auto-delete timer for self-destructing posts.
- 🗑️ Manually delete your posts whenever you want.

### 🔍 Anonymous Chat — Meet New People
Bored of your usual social circle? Find a random chat partner instantly, without giving away who you are.

- ⚡ Fast, automated matchmaking.
- 🔄 Type `/next` to skip and find a new partner anytime.
- 🎭 100% hidden identity from start to finish.
- 📎 Share text, photos, videos, stickers, and voice notes freely.

### 🎁 Referral System
Invite your friends using your personal referral link, and both of you will score extra free menfess quotas. The more, the merrier!

### 🚨 Admin Support & Reporting
Encounter a toxic user? Report them directly from the chat. Need assistance? Reach out to the admins without compromising your anonymity.
